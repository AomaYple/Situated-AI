## 简体中文

[h1]Situated AI[/h1]

[h2]目标[/h2]

Situated AI 让每个国家基于自身处境和可观测信息，在原版机制内持续做出符合自身约束与利益的相对最优决策。

Mod 从原版提供的机会出发，读取具体对象的实际情况，评估行动的需求、收益和成本，再对原版 AI 的局部倾向作有界调整。同一原因的不同强度档避免重复计分，独立收益和成本可以共同影响同一行动；国家仍由原版 AI 比较和执行行动。

各领域根据当前事实重新判断，不按国家名称写死历史路线，也不依靠全国排他路线锁住其他领域。财政风险独立保留进入与退出之间的迟滞；已接入模块在关闭或核心输入不可用时保留原版行为。

[h2]内容[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]财政、建设与产业：[/b]根据偿付压力、行政状况、投入短缺和市场条件，调整相关建设与经济倾向。行政修复有自己的需求入口；正常融资与违约压力分别处理。
[*][b]科技与教育：[/b]针对具体科技、大学、教育法律与制度，区分基础需求、已研发技术和实际投入准备；低识字不会阻止基础发展，研究倾向也不冒充技术已经采用。
[*][b]政治与社会：[/b]根据候选法律的实际边际收益、当前制度、政治运动、阻力与转换损失，调整改革、医疗、福利和行政倾向，保留原版支持、资格和失败流程。
[*][b]贸易与公司：[/b]联系具体商品、材料配方、州与市场条件，分别评估供给机会和投入成本；不同材料的成本能够同时参与，同一材料的不同压力档避免重复计分。
[*][b]外交、宗属与安全：[/b]依据真实目标、战争、偿付能力与已有承诺，协调外交支持、军备、海军和付费动员；军需需求与独立成本同时参与，原版继续决定谈判、可达性和执行。
[*][b]处境行动与跨域联动：[/b]教育、政治、产业和安全需求可以同时存在。跨域反馈读取已生效的法律、实际制度和当前识字等事实；行动评分较高不代表已经执行，条件退出时只撤回相关增量。
[*][b]有界调整与原版回退：[/b]成本影响相关增量或替代路径；低行政、低合法性等条件不会成为全部发展的共同前置。信息和影响范围以实际接入接口为限，关闭模块或核心输入不可用时保留原版行为。
[/list]

Mod 不赠送资源、不强制行动，也不绕过原版资格、成本、前置、政治支持、随机、拒绝与执行流程；玩家决策路径保持原版。

[h2]兼容性[/h2]

兼容性以锁定的 Victoria 3 原版为基线，使用 Windows、macOS 和 Linux 的标准 Mod 安装方式。Mod 使用原版 AI 消费者和原版执行流程，不替换整个 AI，不运行 Python，也不携带探针、日志或缓存。

与原版一起使用时不要求改变正常玩法。建议使用独立 playset，并在新局或备份存档上启用。

其他 Mod 如果覆盖相同的 AI 策略、法律、建筑、科技、外交、军事或殖民文件，可能产生正常的内容冲突；这类组合不在兼容保证范围内。

工程检查核对已接入机制、生成脚本和包合同；长期国家行为、数值平衡、游戏内性能、语言呈现和实际游玩体验仍需用户在固定包上验证。

## English

[h1]Situated AI[/h1]

[h2]Goal[/h2]

Situated AI helps each country make context-sensitive decisions within Victoria 3's original mechanics, using observable information to weigh its own constraints and interests.

The mod starts with choices offered by the game, reads the circumstances of the specific objects involved, and evaluates an action's needs, benefits and costs. It then applies a bounded adjustment to the relevant AI preference. Different intensity levels of the same cause avoid double counting; independent benefits and costs can contribute together. The original AI still compares and executes actions.

Each area responds to current facts. Countries have no scripted historical path based on their name, and no exclusive national route blocks unrelated areas. Fiscal risk retains separate entry and recovery thresholds. Connected modules preserve vanilla behavior when disabled or when their core inputs are unavailable.

[h2]Content[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Finance, construction and industry:[/b] Adjusts relevant construction and economic preferences using solvency pressure, administrative conditions, input shortages and market conditions. Administrative repair has its own demand signal; normal financing and default pressure are treated separately.
[*][b]Technology and education:[/b] Evaluates specific technologies, universities, education laws and institutions, separating basic needs, researched technologies and actual input readiness. Low literacy does not block basic development, and a research preference does not imply that a technology has already been adopted.
[*][b]Politics and society:[/b] Uses a candidate law's marginal effects, current institutions, political movements, resistance and transition losses to adjust reform, healthcare, welfare and administrative preferences, while preserving vanilla support, eligibility and failure processes.
[*][b]Trade and companies:[/b] Connects specific goods, input recipes, states and markets to supply opportunities and input costs. Costs from different materials can contribute together; different pressure levels for the same material avoid double counting.
[*][b]Diplomacy, subjects and security:[/b] Coordinates diplomatic support, armaments, naval choices and paid mobilization options using actual targets, wars, solvency and existing commitments. Military needs and independent costs contribute together; vanilla still determines negotiations, reachability and execution.
[*][b]Contextual actions and links between areas:[/b] Educational, political, industrial and security needs can coexist. Links read enacted laws, actual institutions and current literacy. A higher action score does not mean the action has happened; when a condition ends, only its related contribution is removed.
[*][b]Bounded adjustments and vanilla fallback:[/b] Costs affect relevant contributions or alternative paths. Low administrative capacity or legitimacy does not become a shared prerequisite for all development. Information and influence are limited to the interfaces actually connected; disabled modules or unavailable core inputs preserve vanilla behavior.
[/list]

The mod grants no resources, forces no actions, and does not bypass vanilla eligibility, costs, prerequisites, political support, randomness, refusal or execution. Player decision paths retain vanilla behavior.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

Compatibility is based on the locked Victoria 3 vanilla baseline, using the standard Mod installation structure on Windows, macOS and Linux. The mod uses vanilla AI consumers and execution processes. It does not replace the entire AI, run Python in the game, or include probes, logs or caches.

Using it with vanilla does not require a different way to play. A separate playset and a new game or backed-up save are recommended.

Other mods that override the same AI strategies, laws, buildings, technologies, diplomatic, military or colonial files may cause content conflicts. Those combinations are outside the compatibility guarantee.

Engineering checks cover connected mechanics, generated scripts and package contracts. Long-term country behavior, balance, in-game performance, language presentation and the playing experience still need player validation with a fixed package.
