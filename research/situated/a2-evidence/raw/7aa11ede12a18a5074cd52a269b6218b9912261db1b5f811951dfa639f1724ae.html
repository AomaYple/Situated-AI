Description
Situated AI

Goal

Situated AI helps each country make context-sensitive decisions within Victoria 3's original mechanics, using observable information to weigh its own constraints and interests.

The mod starts with choices offered by the game, reads the circumstances of the specific objects involved, and evaluates an action's needs, benefits and costs. It then applies a bounded adjustment to the relevant AI preference. Different intensity levels of the same cause avoid double counting; independent benefits and costs can contribute together. The original AI still compares and executes actions.

Each area responds to current facts. Countries have no scripted historical path based on their name, and no exclusive national route blocks unrelated areas. Fiscal risk retains separate entry and recovery thresholds. Connected modules preserve vanilla behavior when disabled or when their core inputs are unavailable.

Content

  • Finance, construction and industry: Adjusts relevant construction and economic preferences using solvency pressure, administrative conditions, input shortages and market conditions. Administrative repair has its own demand signal; normal financing and default pressure are treated separately.
  • Technology and education: Evaluates specific technologies, universities, education laws and institutions, separating basic needs, researched technologies and actual input readiness. Low literacy does not block basic development, and a research preference does not imply that a technology has already been adopted.
  • Politics and society: Uses a candidate law's marginal effects, current institutions, political movements, resistance and transition losses to adjust reform, healthcare, welfare and administrative preferences, while preserving vanilla support, eligibility and failure processes.
  • Trade and companies: Connects specific goods, input recipes, states and markets to supply opportunities and input costs. Costs from different materials can contribute together; different pressure levels for the same material avoid double counting.
  • Diplomacy, subjects and security: Coordinates diplomatic support, armaments, naval choices and paid mobilization options using actual targets, wars, solvency and existing commitments. Military needs and independent costs contribute together; vanilla still determines negotiations, reachability and execution.
  • Contextual actions and links between areas: Educational, political, industrial and security needs can coexist. Links read enacted laws, actual institutions and current literacy. A higher action score does not mean the action has happened; when a condition ends, only its related contribution is removed.
  • Bounded adjustments and vanilla fallback: Costs affect relevant contributions or alternative paths. Low administrative capacity or legitimacy does not become a shared prerequisite for all development. Information and influence are limited to the interfaces actually connected; disabled modules or unavailable core inputs preserve vanilla behavior.

The mod grants no resources, forces no actions, and does not bypass vanilla eligibility, costs, prerequisites, political support, randomness, refusal or execution. Player decision paths retain vanilla behavior.

Compatibility

Compatibility is based on the locked Victoria 3 vanilla baseline, using the standard Mod installation structure on Windows, macOS and Linux. The mod uses vanilla AI consumers and execution processes. It does not replace the entire AI, run Python in the game, or include probes, logs or caches.

Using it with vanilla does not require a different way to play. A separate playset and a new game or backed-up save are recommended.

Other mods that override the same AI strategies, laws, buildings, technologies, diplomatic, military or colonial files may cause content conflicts. Those combinations are outside the compatibility guarantee.

Engineering checks cover connected mechanics, generated scripts and package contracts. Long-term country behavior, balance, in-game performance, language presentation and the playing experience still need player validation with a fixed package.
